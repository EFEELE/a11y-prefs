Text

Body copy for Text size and Text spacing. The spacing criterion asks for a line height of 1.5, 0.12em between letters and 0.16em between words; level two of the panel applies exactly that. This paragraph also carries an internal link and an external one that opens in a new tab, for Highlight links and Mark new-tab links.

This paragraph is justified, which is what Align to start undoes. Justified text opens uneven rivers of white space between words and is harder to track for readers with dyslexia, which is why WCAG 1.4.8 advises against it. Long enough to actually show the effect once the column is narrow.
